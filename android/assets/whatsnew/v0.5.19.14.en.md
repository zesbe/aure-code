New: **Ask Aure from any app.**

- Select text in WhatsApp, Chrome, a PDF or any other app and pick **Ask Aure** in the text menu. The Aure panel opens over that app, with no app switching.
- One tap to **Explain, Translate, Summarize, Improve writing, Draft a reply, Explain code or Fix code**. Aure picks the most likely one for you.
- Answers stream in as they're written. **Ask a follow-up**, **Replace text** in the original box, **Copy**, **Share** or **Continue in Aure**.
- Safe: Claude gets no tools in this mode, so text from another app can't make your device do anything.
