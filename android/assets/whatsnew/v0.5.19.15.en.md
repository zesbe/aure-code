**Ask Aure now works with WhatsApp too.**

- WhatsApp messages don't use Android's text menu, so: **long-press the message → Copy**, then open Ask Aure from the **Quick Settings tile** (pull down the shade) or the **app icon shortcut** (long-press Aure Code → *Ask copied text*).
- **Share → Ask Aure** is available in apps that can share text.
- Nothing copied? Type your question straight into the panel.
