**ElevenLabs voices for read-aloud.**

- Does the system voice sound robotic? Add your ElevenLabs API key in **Settings → Device & permissions → Read-aloud voice**. Replies, the 🔊 Read aloud button and Voice chat then use natural ElevenLabs voices.
- Pick a voice from your account and a model (Flash v2.5 is fast and cheap, Multilingual v2 is most natural), then tap *Preview*.
- The key stays in the phone's gateway. If credits run out or there's no internet, it falls back to the system voice.
