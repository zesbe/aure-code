**More human-sounding free voices.**

- Aure now automatically picks the **best** Google voice for your language and prefers **Natural** voices when online. It used to get the basic, most robotic one.
- Choose a voice yourself in **Settings → Device & permissions → Read-aloud voice** and tap *Preview*. **Get more voices** opens Google's voice downloads.
- ElevenLabs (paid) is now a hidden optional extra.
